## A项: 财务闭环验证

### 测试文件

### 测试场景

#### Test 1: `test_order_confirm_pay_fulfill_ledger_entries`
**场景**: 下单 $100 → 确认 → 支付 → 发货

| 步骤 | 状态转换 | Ledger 操作 | 验证 |
|------|----------|-------------|------|
| 1 | DRAFT → CONFIRMED | Debit RECEIVABLE +100, Credit REVENUE -100 | ✅ RECEIVABLE=+100, REVENUE=-100 |
| 2 | CONFIRMED → PAID | Debit CASH +100, Credit RECEIVABLE -100 | ✅ CASH=+100, RECEIVABLE=0 (net) |
| 3 | PAID → FULFILLED | (无 Ledger 操作) | ✅ Status=FULFILLED |
| 4 | 全量验证 | 4条 Ledger 条目，总和=0 | ✅ 双重记账平衡 |

#### Test 2: `test_ledger_entries_are_immutable`
**场景**: 尝试 UPDATE ledger_entries → 被 DB trigger 阻止

#### Test 3: `test_inventory_deduction_gap_documented`
**场景**: 验证 FULFILLED 状态不触发库存扣减（已知 GAP）

## D项: Docker 启动测试

### Healthcheck 检查 ✅

| Service | Healthcheck 命令 | Interval | Timeout | Retries |
|---------|-----------------|----------|---------|---------|
| `postgres` | `pg_isready -U ${POSTGRES_USER} -d ${POSTGRES_DB}` | 10s | 5s | 5 |

**结论**: 所有4个 Service 均定义了 healthcheck。✅

### 源码挂载检查 ✅

| Service | Volumes | 是否源码挂载 |
|---------|---------|-------------|
| `postgres` | `postgres_data:/var/lib/postgresql/data` | ❌ Named volume |
| `postgres` | `./database/init.sql:...initdb.d/init.sql:ro` | ❌ Init 脚本 (只读) |
| `redis` | `redis_data:/data` | ❌ Named volume |
| `backend` | (无 volumes) | ✅ 无挂载 |
| `gateway` | `./nginx/gateway.conf:...default.conf:ro` | ❌ 配置文件 (只读) |

**结论**: 无本地源码目录挂载 (如 `./backend:/app`)。✅

### 依赖启动顺序 ✅

```yaml

  postgres: condition: service_healthy
  redis: condition: service_healthy
gateway.depends_on:
  backend: condition: service_healthy
```

**结论**: 使用 `service_healthy` 条件确保正确启动顺序。✅
